🎯 WHAT THIS APP DOES
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

💰 PRICE RECOMMENDATION
   Input:  Listing details + Date
   Output: 3 pricing strategies (Conservative, Recommended, Aggressive)
   Model:  LightGBM Regressor (R² = 0.72)

📈 OCCUPANCY FORECAST
   Input:  Listing details + Current price + Date
   Output: Booking probability (0-100%) with insights
   Model:  LightGBM Classifier (AUC = 0.85)

📊 MARKET ANALYTICS
   Shows: Day patterns, room types, seasonality, price ranges
   Helps: Understand market trends and opportunities
